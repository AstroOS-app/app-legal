Corkivo data deletion

Corkivo currently has no account sign-in or cloud sync. Your tasting notes, ratings and cellar are stored on your device; label photos and recognized text are processed on the device and are not uploaded. Corkivo also sends app-use events, such as opens, scans, searches and subscription events, to SundayTech's Supabase backend and its analytics provider, PostHog. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google.

Remove data stored on your device

Uninstall Corkivo to remove its locally stored notes, ratings, cellar and settings from that device. Uninstalling does not remove previously collected analytics events or cancel a store subscription.

Request deletion of analytics data

Email sundaytechventures@gmail.com with the subject Corkivo data deletion. Tell us that you want your app-use analytics deleted. If you have an app ID available, include it to help locate your events; do not send a password, payment details, photos or tasting notes. We will help identify the events associated with your request and explain if we need more information to find them. Without a usable identifier, we may be unable to match anonymous events to your device.

Store subscriptions are managed separately in Apple or Google account settings. Deleting Corkivo data does not cancel a subscription.
