75Rox account deletion

In 75Rox, open Profile > Delete account and confirm the deletion. The app deletes your account and associated profile, workout, readiness, program, social connection, target event, checkout, entitlement and store purchase records. It also removes the onboarding draft from this device and signs you out.

For help, email support@75rox.com.

Store subscriptions are managed separately and are not cancelled by deleting your account.
