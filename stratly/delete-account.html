Stratly data deletion

Stratly has no account system. Your strategies and paper trades are stored on your device. If you use background alerts, the alerts server also stores a randomly generated device ID, a push token and your strategy rules.

Delete your data

  1. While online, open Stratly Settings and turn background alerts off. This requests removal of your device ID, push token and strategy rules from the alerts server. If you were offline, reconnect and repeat this step.
  2. In Settings, select Delete my data and confirm. This clears strategies and paper trades from this device and also attempts to remove any remaining alert registration. An offline or failed server request may leave the registration in place, so complete the previous step online before uninstalling.

Uninstalling the app alone removes local data but may leave a registered push token on the alerts server. If you cannot use the app or need help, email sundaytechventures@gmail.com with your request. We will help identify what can be removed; do not send a password or payment details.

Store subscriptions are managed separately. Clearing data does not cancel a subscription.
